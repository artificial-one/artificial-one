Partnership formats

  • Affiliate: performance-based coverage using a unique tracking link.
  • Sponsored: clearly labelled paid placement with editorial independence.
  • Exclusive offer: a reader discount, extended trial, bonus or special plan.
  • Product access: temporary access for testing; access does not guarantee publication.

What makes an offer publishable

  • ✓ Clear benefit for a defined group of AI-tool buyers
  • ✓ Verifiable product, pricing and offer terms
  • ✓ A trackable destination controlled by the partner or affiliate platform
  • ✓ Permission to use approved product assets and factual claims
  • ✓ Honest disclosure of limitations, expiry dates and commercial terms
