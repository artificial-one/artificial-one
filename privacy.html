Privacy, without the fog.

Artificial.One publishes AI-tool research, calculators and partner offers. We collect only the limited information needed to operate the site, measure useful content and manage connected publishing channels.

Contact and data requests
Email hello@artificial.one to ask what information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern.

Website and affiliate measurement

We may process basic technical and aggregate interaction information such as page visits, referring source, campaign parameters, landing page, and clicks on internal or affiliate links. This helps us understand which guides are useful and attribute commissions. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through these measurements.

Information you send us

If you email us or submit a partner enquiry, we process the information you provide so we can answer and maintain the business relationship. We do not sell this information.

LinkedIn and other connected publishing services

Artificial.One currently uses connected services to publish its own content. If future LinkedIn Community Management access is approved and enabled, the integration may process identifiers, names, profile images, public post or comment text, reactions, and aggregate engagement metrics solely to display and manage interactions involving Artificial.One’s authorized LinkedIn Page.

We will not use LinkedIn data to build advertising profiles, sell personal information, scrape unrelated feeds, or send automated connection invitations. Social data retained by our application will be minimized, protected, and deleted when it is no longer needed for the authorized management purpose or when a valid deletion request is received.

Service providers and affiliate destinations

We use hosting, analytics, email-delivery and social-network providers to run the service. Affiliate links take you to a third-party website whose own privacy policy applies. Partners may use referral identifiers or cookies to attribute a purchase to Artificial.One.

Retention and security

We keep business correspondence and operational records only as long as reasonably required for their purpose, legal obligations and fraud prevention. Connected-service tokens are stored as encrypted deployment secrets, not in public website files. Access is limited to the automation that needs it.

Your choices

You may avoid affiliate links, block browser storage through your browser, and contact us to exercise rights available under applicable privacy law. We will verify requests when necessary to protect the person concerned.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service or connected integrations change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are material.
