Google's Gemini Mac App Adds Background Task Handling

Google's new Gemini Mac app allows AI to handle desktop tasks in the background, improving productivity while users work.

The Decoder · Nov 20, 2026

What happened

  • Gemini can run Mac apps and perform background tasks.
  • Users can control what apps Gemini accesses.
  • Gemini handles tasks like photo editing and form filling.

Why it matters

This advancement could revolutionize productivity by allowing AI to handle repetitive tasks in the background, freeing users to focus on more complex work. It also highlights Google's ongoing efforts to improve AI's integration with desktop environments.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Google's Gemini is getting more desktopy, but it's still a bit of a wild ride. The 'Computer Use' feature is a step towards seamless AI integration, though it's not without its quirks.

Who should care

  • Productivity enthusiasts
  • AI researchers
  • Tech companies

What to do next

  1. Test the new feature with personal tasks
  2. Monitor for privacy concerns
  3. Stay updated on AI advancements

Keep in mind

The feature is limited to English in the US and may have privacy implications due to background access.
