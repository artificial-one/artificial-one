Anthropic Founders To Cash Out $250M in IPO

The seven co-founders of Anthropic plan to sell shares in its upcoming IPO, which could result in each receiving around $250 million. The company's valuation is expected to reach $1.4 trillion, and the founders will retain control despite their significant financial gain.

TechCrunch AI · Nov 10, 2026

What happened

  • Anthropic founders will each cash out about $250M in IPO
  • The IPO price range is set between $70 and $78 per share
  • Founders will retain control of the company despite their financial gain

Why it matters

The significant financial gain for Anthropic's founders highlights the immense value of the company's upcoming IPO. Despite their substantial payout, the founders retain control, showing that their vision and leadership are still central to the company's future. This event underscores the growing importance of AI in the tech industry and the potential for massive wealth creation in this sector.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The $250M payout is a clear sign of Anthropic's value, but it's also a reminder that even with big money, the founders still have a say in the company's direction. It's a win for both the founders and the AI space, but not without some underlying tension.

Keep in mind

The IPO price could be higher than the initial range, affecting the founders' actual gains. Also, the founders' stake is a fraction of the total equity, so their control is more about voting rights than ownership.
