NVIDIA Launches New Open Models for Interactive World Simulation

NVIDIA has released four new open models for interactive world simulation, designed to enhance robotics and autonomous driving research. These models include tools for generating multi-sensor data and simulating driving scenarios.

Hugging Face · Nov 05, 2026

What happened

  • NVIDIA has added four new open-weight checkpoints to its Cosmos 3 model family
  • The models are designed for generating interactive world simulation and multi-sensor driving scenes
  • Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Auto generates synchronized camera and LiDAR observations for driving simulation

Why it matters

These new models offer researchers and developers tools to create more realistic simulated environments, crucial for advancing robotics and autonomous driving technologies. By providing open-source models, NVIDIA is fostering collaboration and innovation in the field.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 These models are a step forward in making simulated worlds more interactive and realistic, but the challenge remains in ensuring they accurately support policy learning and evaluation.

Who should care

  • Robotics researchers
  • Autonomous driving developers
  • AI model creators

What to do next

  1. Explore the open-source models for simulation research
  2. Post-train the models on your own data
  3. Contribute improvements to the model development process

Keep in mind

The models are still in development and may have limitations in their ability to support policy learning and evaluation.
