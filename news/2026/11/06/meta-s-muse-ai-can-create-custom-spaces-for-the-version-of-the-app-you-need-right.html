Meta's Muse AI Introduces Custom 'Spaces' for App-Like Experiences

Meta's Muse AI now allows users to create custom, app-like experiences within its assistant through 'spaces,' enabling features like shopping hubs and school dashboards. The feature is set to roll out to all users.

The Verge AI · Nov 06, 2026

What happened

  • Meta's Muse AI can create custom 'spaces' for the version of the app you need right now
  • Muse allows users to build customized, app-like experiences within its assistant
  • Users can interact with their spaces by checking things off, adding notes, and making edits

Why it matters

Meta's Muse AI is expanding its capabilities to offer more personalized and interactive experiences. The 'spaces' feature allows users to tailor their interactions with the AI, potentially improving productivity and user engagement with the platform.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Meta’s Muse AI is getting a bit more app-like, but it’s still a bit of a stretch. The 'spaces' feature is a step towards personalization, but it's not clear how much this will improve the user experience beyond the basics.

Who should care

  • AI developers
  • Tech enthusiasts
  • Users of Meta products

What to do next

  1. Explore the new 'spaces' feature in Muse AI
  2. Test the feature with different use cases
  3. Provide feedback to Meta on the usability of the feature

Keep in mind

The feature is still in its early stages and may have limitations in terms of functionality and user interface.
