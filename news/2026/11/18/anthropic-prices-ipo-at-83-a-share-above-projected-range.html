Anthropic's IPO Surpasses Record with $81.3 Billion Proceeds

Anthropic's IPO at $83 per share exceeds expectations, generating $81.3 billion in proceeds and setting a new record for the largest IPO by proceeds. The company is valued at $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, with major investors including Amazon and Google holding significant stakes.

TechCrunch AI · Nov 18, 2026

What happened

  • Anthropic's IPO price of $83 per share exceeds its initial range.
  • The IPO generates $81.3 billion in proceeds, surpassing SpaceX’s record.
  • The company is valued at $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

Why it matters

Anthropic's record-breaking IPO highlights the immense value placed on AI companies despite market volatility. The valuation and proceeds underscore investor confidence in AI technology, while also revealing the significant stakes held by major tech firms like Amazon and Google.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Anthropic’s $1.5 trillion valuation is a staggering testament to AI’s potential, but it’s also a reminder that even the most optimistic projections can be swayed by market shifts. The IPO’s success may set a new benchmark for private AI companies, though it’s not without its risks.
