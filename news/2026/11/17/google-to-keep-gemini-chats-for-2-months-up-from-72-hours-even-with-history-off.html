Google Extends Gemini Chat Retention to Two Months

Google is increasing the retention period for Gemini chats from 72 hours to two months, aiming to enhance safety and security. The change will roll out globally in the coming weeks, with users able to separate chat history from AI training.

TechCrunch AI · Nov 17, 2026

What happened

  • Google is extending the retention of temporary Gemini chats to two months.
  • The change aims to improve safety and security as AI systems become more capable.
  • Users can now separate chat history from AI training in new settings.

Why it matters

This update highlights Google's efforts to balance user privacy with system safety. By extending chat retention, Google is preparing for potential system failures and policy violations, while giving users more control over their data. It also reflects the growing complexity of AI systems and the need for robust security measures.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Google is trying to be both safe and flexible with its AI. By keeping chats longer, they’re ready for emergencies, but it’s a bit odd that they’re not using this data for training. Maybe they’re trying to reassure users about privacy while still being cautious.

Who should care

  • AI developers
  • Privacy advocates
  • Tech companies
  • Users concerned about data retention

What to do next

  1. Review Google's privacy settings for Gemini
  2. Stay informed about AI updates from Google
  3. Consider the implications of data retention on privacy
  4. Contact Google if you have concerns about chat history

Keep in mind

The change could raise privacy concerns if not handled carefully. Users might worry about their conversations being stored longer than intended.
