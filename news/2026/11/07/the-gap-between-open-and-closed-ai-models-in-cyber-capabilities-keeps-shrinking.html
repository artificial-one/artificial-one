Open AI Models Close Cyber Capabilities Gap with Closed Models

The gap between open and closed AI models in cyber capabilities is shrinking as open models perform well in simulated attacks and are more cost-effective. Analysts warn that defenders have less time to respond as open models can no longer be restricted.

The Decoder · Nov 07, 2026

What happened

  • Open AI models can break into realistic corporate networks on their own
  • Open models are more cost-effective than closed models for cyber testing
  • Safety measures on open models are largely ineffective

Why it matters

As open AI models become more capable and affordable, the threat landscape for cyber security is evolving. Defenders must adapt quickly to counter the growing capabilities of these models, which can now access and exploit vulnerabilities without restrictions.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Open models are closing the gap in cyber capabilities, but defenders are running out of time. The cost advantage is a double-edged sword—more accessible tools mean more potential for exploitation.

Who should care

  • Cybersecurity professionals
  • AI researchers
  • Corporate IT managers

What to do next

  1. Monitor AI model capabilities and update security protocols regularly
  2. Conduct regular vulnerability assessments using open models for testing
  3. Invest in AI-driven threat detection systems
  4. Collaborate with AI safety researchers to improve model safeguards

Keep in mind

The findings highlight the urgent need for improved security measures and proactive vulnerability detection as open models become more prevalent in cyber testing.
