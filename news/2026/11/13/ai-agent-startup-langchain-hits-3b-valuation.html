LangChain Valued at $3B with 7,000 Customers

LangChain, an AI agent startup, has achieved a $3B valuation with 7,000 customers using its platform agnostic agent management tool LangSmith. The company raised $300 million in Series C funding led by CapitalG.

TechCrunch AI · Nov 13, 2026

What happened

  • LangChain has a $3B valuation after raising $300 million in Series C.
  • LangChain has 7,000 customers using its agent management tool LangSmith.
  • LangChain's valuation more than doubled from $1.25 billion to $3B.

Why it matters

The $3B valuation highlights the growing interest in AI agent management tools, with LangChain leading the market. Its platform agnostic approach and focus on letting businesses own their intelligence could position it as a key player in the AI ecosystem.

The Elephant take

🐘 🦘 LangChain's $3B valuation is impressive, but the competition is fierce. With 7,000 customers, it's clear the company has found its niche in managing AI agents, though it faces challenges from other startups like Mintive.

Who should care

  • AI investors
  • TechCrunch readers
  • Startups in AI space

What to do next

  1. Monitor LangChain's customer growth and product development
  2. Evaluate the competition in the AI agent management space
  3. Consider the implications of a $3B valuation for the AI industry

Keep in mind

The valuation is based on a recent Series C round, and the market for AI agent tools remains highly competitive.
