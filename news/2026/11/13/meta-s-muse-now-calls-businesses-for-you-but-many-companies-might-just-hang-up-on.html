Meta's Muse Calls Businesses but Firms May Hang Up on AI

Meta's AI agent Muse can call businesses on behalf of users, but many companies may hang up when they detect an AI on the line. The feature is available to paying subscribers and has over 10 million users.

The Decoder · Nov 13, 2026

What happened

  • Meta's Muse can call businesses on behalf of users
  • Many businesses hang up when they detect an AI on the line
  • Meta's Muse has over 10 million users

Why it matters

The ability of AI to call businesses raises concerns about detection and trust. As AI becomes more realistic, businesses may struggle to distinguish between human and AI calls, potentially impacting customer service and data privacy.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Meta's Muse is a double-edged sword: while it streamlines business calls, the risk of being detected as AI could undermine its effectiveness. The challenge is balancing convenience with trust.

Who should care

  • Businesses
  • AI developers
  • Customer service professionals

What to do next

  1. Test AI call detection methods
  2. Improve AI transparency
  3. Enhance user education on AI capabilities

Keep in mind

The detection issue highlights the need for clear identification of AI interactions in customer service.
