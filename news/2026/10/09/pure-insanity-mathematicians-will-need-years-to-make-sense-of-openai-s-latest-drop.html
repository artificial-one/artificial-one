Mathematicians Struggle to Understand OpenAI's Massive AI-Generated Results

OpenAI released over 400 mathematical results, sparking awe and anxiety among mathematicians who fear the sheer scale of the work will take years to comprehend.

The Verge AI · Oct 09, 2026

What happened

  • Mathematicians described the release as 'pure insanity'.
  • OpenAI released nearly 400 AI-generated mathematical results.
  • Many mathematicians struggle to digest the sheer volume of work.

Why it matters

The release of such a massive amount of AI-generated mathematical results could reshape the field, but the sheer scale and uncertainty of verification may leave mathematicians in confusion for years.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The sheer volume of OpenAI's mathematical output is overwhelming, with some fearing it could take years to understand. It’s a surreal moment for the field, as AI starts to outpace human mathematicians in generating results.

Who should care

  • Mathematicians
  • AI researchers
  • Academia

What to do next

  1. Review the GitHub repository for guidance on navigating the massive collection.
  2. Assess the verification status of each result before accepting them as valid.
  3. Stay prepared for potential future releases from OpenAI.

Keep in mind

The results are at different stages of verification, and not all have been formalized.
