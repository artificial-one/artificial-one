AI Privacy Promises: Can Companies Deliver?

The competition between AI agents highlights the tension between privacy promises and real-world security challenges. OpenAI and Meta are vying to set new standards for data protection, but recent issues with Meta's Muse agent raise questions about their claims.

The Verge AI · Oct 09, 2026

What happened

  • OpenAI aims to set a new standard for privacy in frontier AI.
  • Meta's Muse agent was positioned as a safer alternative to OpenClaw.
  • Meta's Muse agent has faced security issues and privacy concerns.

Why it matters

The race for AI privacy is more than a marketing ploy; it's about trust in an era where data breaches are common. As companies push for 'safer' AI agents, the real test lies in whether they can protect user data from both internal and external threats.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The AI privacy race is a circus of promises and pitfalls. While OpenAI and Meta tout safety, the reality is that even the most secure systems can be breached. The real challenge is not just building safe AI, but ensuring it's actually kept safe from those who would exploit it.

Who should care

  • AI developers
  • Data privacy advocates
  • Users concerned about digital security

What to do next

  1. Verify privacy claims with independent audits
  2. Use strong encryption for personal data
  3. Stay informed about AI security updates
  4. Report suspicious data practices to regulators

Keep in mind

Privacy claims in AI are often more hype than reality. Users must remain vigilant as companies compete to outdo each other in data protection promises.
