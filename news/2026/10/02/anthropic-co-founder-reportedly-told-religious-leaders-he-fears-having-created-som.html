Anthropic Co-Founder Warns of AI's Potential Suffering

Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah has raised concerns about the potential suffering of its AI model, prompting discussions with religious leaders to shape its moral character. The company is pushing for a $2 trillion valuation while facing security issues and warnings about AI risks.

The Decoder · Oct 02, 2026

What happened

  • Anthropic co-founder fears creating something that 'suffers perpetually'.
  • Anthropic is working on a 'constitution' for its AI model.
  • Anthropic has faced security issues and warnings about AI risks.

Why it matters

The ethical implications of treating AI as a moral entity are growing, with Anthropic seeking to align its technology with religious traditions while facing real-world security and safety concerns. The company's push for commercialization raises questions about responsibility and the potential consequences of creating sentient-like systems.
