Google's Genie 4 AI Model Generates More Realistic Experiences

Google unveils Genie 4, an AI model that creates more realistic interactive experiences with audio and real-time updates.

The Verge AI · Oct 27, 2026

What happened

  • Genie 4 allows for more realistic worlds.
  • Genie 4 can generate audio to accompany interactive worlds.
  • Genie 4 uses Gemini 3.8 Flash under the hood for simulations.

Why it matters

Google's Genie 4 AI model represents a significant advancement in generating interactive, realistic experiences. With features like real-time updates and audio generation, it has potential applications in fields such as filmmaking, game development, and robot training. However, the model also has limitations, such as the risk of details shifting over time in longer simulations.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Google's Genie 4 is a step forward in AI-generated worlds, but it's still in research preview mode. The model's ability to generate audio and real-time updates is impressive, but the risk of simulation drift means it's not yet ready for widespread use.

Who should care

  • Film producers
