Nvidia invests in Indian AI startup to optimize GPU usage

Nvidia has invested in an Indian AI startup that helps companies run AI models more efficiently, reducing reliance on GPUs. The startup claims a 14-fold revenue growth in six months and works with major AI firms.

TechCrunch AI · Oct 27, 2026

What happened

  • Nvidia has invested in an Indian AI startup to help companies optimize GPU usage
  • The startup claims a significant revenue growth in six months
  • The startup works with major AI companies to optimize their models

Why it matters

As AI demand grows, optimizing GPU usage becomes critical. Nvidia's investment in Simplismart highlights the need for efficient AI infrastructure solutions that reduce costs and improve performance.
