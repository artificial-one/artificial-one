Microsoft CEO adopts Trump's AI terminology to undermine rivals

Microsoft's Satya Nadella aligns with Trump's preferred language for AI while criticizing OpenAI and Anthropic, suggesting a strategic move to protect Microsoft's business interests.

The Decoder · Oct 11, 2026

What happened

  • Nadella uses 'Super Intelligence' instead of 'Artificial Intelligence'.
  • Nadella frames AI models as insider security threats.
  • Microsoft is replacing OpenAI and Anthropic models with cheaper alternatives.

Why it matters

Nadella's shift in terminology and criticism of rivals may be a strategic move to protect Microsoft's business interests. By aligning with Trump's language, he could be trying to gain political advantage while undermining competitors. This highlights the complex interplay between corporate strategy, political influence, and technological competition.

The Elephant take
