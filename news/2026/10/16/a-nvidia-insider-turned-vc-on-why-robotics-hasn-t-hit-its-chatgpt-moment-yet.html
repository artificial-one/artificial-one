Robotics in 'Lego Era' Before ChatGPT Moment

Amulya Vishwanath, a former NVIDIA executive, argues robotics is still in its early stages, with foundational elements like actuators and safety standards needing development. This contrasts with the belief that robotics could soon reach a 'ChatGPT moment' with general foundation models.

TechCrunch AI · Oct 16, 2026

What happened

  • Robotics is currently in its 'Lego era'.
  • Basic building blocks like actuators and safety standards are still being rebuilt.
  • Some believe robotics could soon have a 'ChatGPT moment'.

Why it matters

The debate over whether robotics is ready for a breakthrough akin to ChatGPT highlights the challenges of integrating complex physical systems with AI. It underscores the need for foundational advancements before practical applications can emerge.

The Elephant take

🐘 🦘 Robotics is still playing with building blocks, not yet crafting the next big thing. The 'Lego era' suggests we're far from the 'ChatGPT moment'—a reminder that AI's physical counterparts are still in their infancy.

Who should care

  • AI investors
  • Robotics developers
  • Tech enthusiasts

What to do next

  1. Invest in foundational robotics research
  2. Monitor advancements in AI-driven physical systems
  3. Stay informed about industry trends and debates

Keep in mind

The 'Lego era' perspective may overlook the rapid progress in specialized robotics and AI integration.
