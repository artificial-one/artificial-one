Michael I. Jordan Criticizes AI Doom Rhetoric as 'Grand Moralizing Performance'

Machine learning pioneer Michael I. Jordan dismisses AI doom rhetoric as 'grand moralizing performance' and criticizes the hype around superintelligence as science fiction. He highlights the naivety of young researchers and the theatrical demands for regulation by tech companies.

The Decoder · Oct 13, 2026

What happened

  • Jordan calls warnings about existential AI threats pure science fiction
  • Jordan views resignations of young AI researchers as a 'grand moralizing performance'
  • Jordan dismisses companies' demands for regulation as 'theater'

Why it matters

Jordan's critique highlights the disconnect between AI hype and reality, warning against the dangers of framing superintelligence as an existential threat. His analysis underscores the need for practical, grounded discourse in AI development rather than sensationalized rhetoric.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Jordan sees AI doom as theater, not science. The 'superintelligence' buzz is more fiction than fact, and the naive young researchers are just another act in a grand performance. It's time to stop pretending AI is a existential threat and focus on real progress.

Who should care

  • AI Researchers
  • Tech Industry Leaders
  • Policy Makers

What to do next

  1. Promote grounded, practical discussions about AI development
  2. Encourage critical thinking about AI hype and rhetoric
  3. Support real-world applications of AI technology
  4. Foster a more nuanced understanding of AI risks and benefits

Keep in mind

Jordan's views may be influenced by his personal and professional experiences with AI research and industry.
