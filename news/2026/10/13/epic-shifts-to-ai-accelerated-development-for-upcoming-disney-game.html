Epic Games Uses AI for Disney Game Development

Epic Games is using AI tools like Anthropic's Claude to accelerate development of an unannounced Disney game, which has been delayed from its original March release window.

The Verge AI · Oct 13, 2026

What happened

  • Epic Games is using AI to generate code and art for a Disney game.
  • The game is now delayed from its original March release window.
  • Epic Games has given developers an 'AI-first' mandate.

Why it matters

The shift to AI-accelerated development in game creation highlights how technology is reshaping entertainment production. Epic Games' move could signal a broader trend in the gaming industry, where AI tools are being used to speed up development and reduce costs. This also comes as Epic faces challenges like layoffs and declining engagement on Fortnite.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋Epic is trying to out-AI the AI with a 'AI-first' mandate, but it's unclear if this will result in better games or just more code. The Disney collaboration adds a layer of complexity, as the company is already investing heavily in Epic.
