Blue Planet III embraces imperfection in AI era

BBC's Blue Planet III is intentionally keeping unpolished shots to prove authenticity, countering the polished look often associated with AI. The series aims to show a more organic version of the oceans by leaving in less-than-perfect footage.

The Verge AI · Oct 24, 2026

What happened

  • Blue Planet III is intentionally keeping unpolished shots to prove authenticity
  • Previous nature series were defined by their polished, almost too good to be real footage
  • The series aims to show a more organic version of the oceans by leaving in less-than-perfect footage

Why it matters

Blue Planet III's decision to embrace imperfection in an AI era highlights a conscious effort to differentiate authenticity from artificiality. By leaving in unpolished shots, the series aims to showcase the raw, organic beauty of the oceans, challenging the perception that AI-generated content is always flawless.

It's a reminder that sometimes, the real thing is more compelling than the perfect one.
