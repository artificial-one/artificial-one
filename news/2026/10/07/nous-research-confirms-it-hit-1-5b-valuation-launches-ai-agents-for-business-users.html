Nous Research hits $1.5B valuation, launches AI agents for businesses

Nous Research, developer of Hermes Agent, raises $90M Series B at $1.5B valuation, aiming to expand into enterprise with customized AI agents.

TechCrunch AI · Oct 07, 2026

What happened

  • Nous Research has raised a $90 million Series B at a $1.5 billion valuation
  • The new financing brings the three-year-old startup’s total funding to $158 million
  • Hermes Agent has been cloned more than 24 million times, driving roughly 2.5% of global AI token usage

Why it matters

The $1.5B valuation highlights the growing interest in AI agents for business applications, with a focus on enterprise solutions and data privacy. This development could signal a shift towards more specialized AI tools tailored for corporate use.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The $1.5B valuation is a big deal, but it's not clear if Hermes Agent will actually deliver the promised enterprise solutions. The numbers are impressive, but the real test is whether the AI agents can handle complex workflows without compromising data security.

Who should care

  • AI investors
  • Enterprise tech leaders
  • Startups in AI space
  • Data privacy advocates

What to do next

  1. Research the company's track record
  2. Evaluate the AI agent's capabilities
  3. Monitor data security practices
  4. Stay updated on AI trends in enterprise

Keep in mind

The valuation may be inflated and the claims about AI token usage are estimates.
