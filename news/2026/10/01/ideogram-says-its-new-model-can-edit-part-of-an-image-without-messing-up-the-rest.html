Ideogram's New Model Offers Precise Image Editing

Ideogram introduces Ideogram 4.5, a model that edits images without affecting other areas. The model is available on the Ideogram platform and through APIs, with partners like Runway and Pika. It offers four quality tiers and native 2K resolution.

The Decoder · Oct 01, 2026

What happened

  • Ideogram's new model can edit part of an image without messing up the rest
  • The model is available now on the Ideogram platform and through the API
  • Partners include Picsart, Runway, Pika, and Leonardo AI

Why it matters

Ideogram's new model addresses a key challenge in image editing by allowing precise modifications without unintended side effects. This could improve efficiency in fields like product photography and interior design, where accuracy is crucial. The model's availability through APIs and partnerships suggests broad adoption potential.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Ideogram's 4.5 model is a step forward in image editing precision, but the hype might overshadow its real-world utility. The 'no messing up' claim is promising, but early tests may not fully validate its claims.

Who should care

Keep in mind
