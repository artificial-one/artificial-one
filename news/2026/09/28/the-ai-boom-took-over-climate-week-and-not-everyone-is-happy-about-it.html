AI Boom Divides Climate Tech Community at Climate Week

The AI boom is reshaping climate tech, with some embracing it as an opportunity and others concerned about its impact on other sectors.

TechCrunch AI · Sep 28, 2026

What happened

  • Climate tech founders and investors are divided by the AI boom's influence.
  • AI-driven data centers are being built to power AI, raising concerns about natural gas usage.
  • Climate tech startups are pivoting to align with AI trends for funding and growth.

Why it matters

The AI boom is reshaping climate tech, creating both opportunities and challenges. While some see it as a way to scale businesses, others worry it's diverting attention from other critical areas of climate action.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The AI boom is a double-edged sword for climate tech. While it’s helping some startups scale, it risks sidelining other vital sectors. It’s a race between growth and purpose.

Who should care

  • Climate tech founders
  • Investors
  • Policy makers
  • Environmentalists

What to do next

  1. Pivot strategies to align with current trends while maintaining core mission
  2. Monitor the long-term impact of AI on climate sectors
  3. Diversify funding sources to avoid over-reliance on AI-driven opportunities
  4. Engage in dialogue to ensure AI integration supports, rather than undermines, climate goals

Keep in mind

The AI-driven climate tech trend may not last forever, but it could provide short-term gains.
