Google Shifts to Agent-Ready Prompt Formats

Google is replacing its Gems with 'Skills' in Gemini chat, aligning with OpenAI and Anthropic's moves toward agent-ready prompt formats. Skills allow users to save and invoke detailed prompts for specific tasks, with support for text, PDFs, and images.

The Decoder · Sep 30, 2026

What happened

  • Google is replacing Gems with 'Skills' in Gemini chat.
  • Skills are detailed prompts for specific tasks that the AI can help write and refine.
  • The Skills format originated with Anthropic and is available as an open standard.

Why it matters

This shift reflects a broader industry trend toward more structured, reusable prompts for AI agents. By adopting a standardized format, Google is aligning with other major players like OpenAI and Anthropic, signaling a move towards more sophisticated AI interaction models.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Google's move to Skills is a subtle but significant step toward more structured AI interactions. It’s not just about replacing one feature with another, but about rethinking how users and AI collaborate on complex tasks.

Who should care

  • AI developers
  • Tech companies
  • Users of Gemini chat

What to do next

  1. Review the new Skills format to understand how it works
  2. Test the new features with a small group of users before full rollout
  3. Monitor the transition period for any issues or user feedback

Keep in mind

The transition from Gems to Skills may take time for users to adapt, and the long-term impact of this shift remains to be seen.
