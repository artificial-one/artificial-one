OpenAI Unveils Dots, Competes with Meta's Muse

OpenAI is launching Dots, an AI agent product that competes with Meta's Muse. Unlike Muse, which is free, Dots will be available to paid ChatGPT subscribers. The company also announced new features and a higher user base for ChatGPT.

The Verge AI · Sep 30, 2026

What happened

  • OpenAI is launching Dots, an AI agent product that competes with Meta's Muse
  • Dots will be available to paid ChatGPT subscribers
  • ChatGPT has surpassed 1.2 billion weekly users

Why it matters

The launch of Dots highlights the competitive landscape in AI agents, with OpenAI responding to Meta's Muse. The availability of Dots to paid subscribers and the growth of ChatGPT's user base indicate a shift in how AI services are being monetized and accessed.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 OpenAI is trying to keep up with Meta's Muse, but it's still behind in the race to make AI agents available to everyone. The company is focusing on paid subscribers, which might not be the best strategy for widespread adoption.

  • Tech investors
  • Regulators
