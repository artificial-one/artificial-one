Google Tests Buying from Flipkart via AI in India

Google is testing an AI feature that allows users to buy products from Flipkart directly through Gemini and AI Mode in India, with a broader rollout planned for October.

TechCrunch AI · Sep 27, 2026

What happened

  • Google is testing an AI feature that lets users buy products from Flipkart directly through Gemini and AI Mode.
  • The test is limited to select users and products, with a broader rollout planned for October.
  • Google has a financial relationship with Flipkart through an investment in 2024.

Why it matters

This test marks Google's expansion into AI-driven commerce, aiming to move beyond product recommendations to direct purchases. It highlights Google's strategic partnership with Flipkart and its efforts to dominate the Indian market through AI technology.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 Google is trying to make AI more than just a chatbot—now it can buy things too. But the test is still limited, and it’s unclear how this will scale beyond India.

What to do next

Keep in mind

The test is in its early stages and may not be fully functional or widely available yet.
