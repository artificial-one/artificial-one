AI Use in Healthcare Linked to Increased Costs

A study by Blue Cross Blue Shield Association found that AI tools in hospitals led to additional healthcare spending, highlighting a growing concern about AI's impact on costs.

TechCrunch AI · Sep 26, 2026

What happened

  • AI tools in hospitals increased healthcare spending by $942M over two years
  • AI is causing a disconnect between medical coding and treatment
  • Insurers and hospitals are at odds over AI's impact on costs

Why it matters

The growing use of AI in healthcare is raising concerns about rising costs and the potential for misalignment between coding practices and actual patient care, signaling a need for careful oversight.

The Elephant take

🐘 鼋 The AI war in healthcare is getting bloodier, with insurers losing out as hospitals use AI to document complex conditions, but no evidence of better care. It's a messy fight with no clear winner.

Who should care

  • Healthcare providers
  • Insurance companies
  • AI developers

What to do next

  1. Monitor AI's impact on healthcare costs
  2. Ensure AI tools improve patient care, not just documentation
  3. Collaborate with insurers to find solutions
  4. Invest in AI ethics and oversight frameworks

Keep in mind

The analysis focuses on coding practices, not actual patient outcomes or treatment quality.
